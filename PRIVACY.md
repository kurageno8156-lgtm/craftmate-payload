# Privacy Policy

CraftMate is a locally-run application. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

## Data Handling

- **API Keys (Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, Ollama):** Stored locally in the .env file on the user's machine. They are never sent to the developer or any third party other than the selected AI provider.
- **Minecraft Connection Info:** Server address, port, and username are stored locally in .env. They are only used to connect to the user's own Minecraft server.
- **Chat Logs and Bot Actions:** Displayed in the local web dashboard and not transmitted externally.

## Third-Party Services

When you use an AI provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, Ollama), your inputs may be sent to that provider according to their respective privacy policies.
